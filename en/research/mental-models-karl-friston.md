## PRIMARY MODELS (home base — 3, verified against dossier)

### 1. Probabilistic Thinking (Bayesian precision-weighting)
This is not one tool among several for Friston — it is the mechanism he reaches for to explain every state change in the self, across every source in the window.

- FEP background: living systems "act as if... constantly trying to confirm [their] own predictions and avoid being surprised by the world."
- REBUS/FIBUS: psychedelics "relax high-precision, high-level priors" and "reliably increase epistemic depth" — but this is explicitly decoupled from truth (FIBUS: "False Insights and Beliefs Under Psychedelics").
- Precision Psychiatry abstract (May 2026): meditation, dissociation, and flow are all to be explained via "the (neuromodulatory) encoding of uncertainty or precision" — i.e., one dial, many settings.
- Beautiful Loop: competing inferences "fight for precision-weighting; only ones that coherently reduce long-run uncertainty 'win.'"

**Sees clearly:** a single, economical, quantitative mechanism that unifies psychedelics, meditation, dissociation, flow, psychiatric illness, and (by extension) AI agency as "variations on one mechanism" — precision-weighting turned up or down at different levels of a belief hierarchy. This is the dossier's own "cleanest one-line summary" of his framework's implication for ego dissolution.

**Misses:** phenomenologically distinct states (a DMT trip vs. jhāna cessation vs. dissociative disorder) get flattened into differences of *degree* on the same parameter rather than differences of *kind*. Stegemann's critique (Nov–Dec 2025) presses exactly here: "precision" risks doing undefined metaphysical work — a fitted parameter dressed as a discovered quantity.

### 2. First Principles Thinking (physics-derivation, not psychological analogy)
Friston consistently refuses to start from folk-psychological categories and instead derives selfhood from formal/physical fundamentals.

- Physics of Sentience talk (Oct 2025): internal/external states "must be conditionally independent" — this conditional independence is what gives a system self-modeling behavior; framed as "a near-physical law rather than a psychological metaphor."
- Physics of "As If" (Dec 2025): defends FEP via renormalization-group theory — neural dynamics can be described "*exactly*... as inference under a generative model, at the appropriate scale," analogized to the moon being exactly spherical at astronomical resolution.
- IAI TV (Sept 2025): frames FEP as unifying physics and psychology outright.

**Sees clearly:** universality — the same derivation applies to a cell, a brain, an institution, or an AI system, because it starts from a scale-free mathematical property (boundedness) rather than a domain-specific psychological category. Lets him dismiss folk-psychological objections as not engaging the "real" (formal) level.

**Misses:** the difference between mathematical isomorphism and metaphysical identity. Stegemann's rebuttal names this directly: "mathematical isomorphism between neural dynamics and Bayesian inference does not establish identity between them; physical properties are not semantic properties just because you can coarse-grain between them" — a category error the first-principles move is structurally prone to, since deriving-from-fundamentals invites treating the derivation as reality rather than description.

### 3. Systems Thinking (Markov blankets, attractors, self-referential loops)
The connective tissue that lets the other two models operate on a well-defined "thing."

- Markov Blanket: "the statistical/informational boundary separating a system's 'internal states' from 'external states'... what individuates a 'thing' — a cell, a person, an institution."
- Attractor/identity: "there's only a very small number of all the states I could be in that are consistent with me living and being alive"; "I can be me in Melbourne, and I can be me in London. But I can only be me between 36.2 and 37.8 degrees centigrade."
- Beautiful Loop: "The Reality Model is being shared with its own underlying architecture, constantly confirming to itself its own existence" — "toroidal epistemicity," a strange loop.

**Sees clearly:** a scale-free vocabulary for individuation and stability that lets him redescribe dissolution/pathology not as loss of a mystical essence but as a describable transition — attractor-switching, blanket reconfiguration, "Bayesian unbinding." This is what makes cessation states mappable onto śūnyatā without abandoning the technical apparatus.

**Misses:** where the boundary gets drawn is itself a choice, and Friston's default placement (organism/brain-bound — "the brain is skull-bound, it lives in the dark") is precisely what draws Thompson's enactivist fire (see Blind Spots). Applied with full rigor, systems thinking should treat blanket-placement as an empirical/pragmatic question; in practice it functions as a near-axiom.

## SECONDARY MODELS (situational, not default)

### 4. Analogical Reasoning
Used to translate the physics register for interdisciplinary/lay audiences, not to generate the theory itself.
- Thermostat: "As soon as you say I and do, you've brought two really big things to the table that the thermostat doesn't have"; "the thermostat has no notion of itself."
- Moon-sphere (renormalization group defense in "Physics of As If").
- "The brain is skull-bound, it lives in the dark."

Clarifies for outside audiences; but the vividness of the analogy sometimes stands in for the rigor of an argument — exactly the "as-if" idealization Stegemann accuses him of denying while using.

### 5. Inversion / Define-by-Negation
Defines "self" by cataloguing what lacks one — most visible in the AI-agency material.
- LLMs are "just a mapping between content and content" (no explicit generative/causal world model).
- Thermostat "has no notion of itself and crucially it doesn't know what it's doing."

Sharp rhetorically and effective against LeCun/Marcus-style claims for LLM agency, but risks a binary (has/doesn't-have a generative model) that doesn't engage gradualist or functionalist accounts of partial agency.

### 6. Falsifiability (engaged defensively, only when challenged)
- Stegemann (Nov 2025): FEP is "framed as unfalsifiable... functioning as mathematical truth rather than an empirical hypothesis."
- Friston's Dec 2025 response reframes the standard (scale-relative exactness) rather than proposing a decisive empirical test.

He has a sophisticated reply ready, but it's a rhetorical parry, not a constraint he applies generatively to his own theorizing.

### 7. Epistemic Humility (mostly via the Laukkonen collaboration)
- FIBUS: "A little bit more flexibility for most humans is a good thing... sometimes that can go terribly wrong."
- Laukkonen: advocates "epistemic humility" over treating psychedelic insights as automatically "valuable and true."

Tempers the theory's totalizing pull — but the humility is exported onto the phenomena being explained (psychedelic insight-content), not imported back onto the FEP/precision framework itself.

## BLIND SPOT MODELS

### A. Enactive / Extended Systems Thinking (boundary-placement blind spot — new model, not in catalog)
The dossier flags this directly via Evan Thompson's March 2026 broadside: "Thinking that perception happens inside the brain is like thinking that flying happens inside the bird's wings or that dancing happens inside the dancer's nervous and musculoskeletal systems" — a direct rebuttal of Friston's own Sept 2025 "skull-bound... lives in the dark" line. Friston's own "Representation Wars: Enacting an Armistice Through Active Inference" shows he knows the gap exists but treats it as a diplomatic boundary dispute rather than adopting the enactive premise. **What this costs the ego-dissolution account:** an entire alternative reading where dissolution is a reorganization of organism-environment coupling, not merely internal reweighting inside a skull-bound model, is foreclosed by default.

### B. Falsifiability as a self-applied constraint
He never turns Falsifiability (present in the reference catalog, and one he can wield defensively — see Secondary #6) into a generative check on FEP itself. No source in the dossier has him proposing what observation would falsify the free-energy principle. This matters for the book's subject specifically: if any observed self-state can be redescribed post hoc as "some setting of precision," the ego-dissolution claims inherit the same unfalsifiability-by-construction problem.

### C. Map vs. Territory, applied reflexively to his own theory
He states the move ("models — including his own theoretical apparatus — are 'parts of my generative model'") but doesn't hold the line at the decisive moment — the moon-is-*exactly*-spherical argument claims exactness, not mere isomorphism. Stegemann's rebuttal is essentially a map/territory objection: coarse-graining an isomorphism into an identity. This is also where critics locate the "smuggled teleology"/homunculus charge — the self the theory claims to dissolve into statistics turns out to be presupposed by the theory's own unexamined "prior preferences, goals, pragmatic value."

### D. Neurophenomenology / first-person data as independent evidence
Notably, Friston himself co-authored the paper (Battaglia, Servajean & Friston, March 2025) that names this gap — arguing integrating neurophenomenology "is needed to resolve" the self-studying-brain paradox — yet his solo and most-collaborative output treats first-person report as something the model explains, not as an independent check on the model. Westerhoff (Buddhist no-self scholar), positioned opposite him on the March 2026 IAI panel, is flagged in the dossier's own synthesis as "the sharpest possible foil" — and that confrontation remains unresolved in the source material.

## HOW THE PRIMARY MODELS COMBINE

The distinctive Friston move is stacking all three at once: **First Principles** licenses treating FEP as a physical law rather than a metaphor, applicable at any scale; **Systems Thinking** supplies the boundary concept (Markov blanket) that specifies *which* bounded thing the physics applies to; **Probabilistic Thinking** supplies the mechanism (precision-weighting) that explains *change over time* within that boundary — construction, defense, and dissolution of the self-model.

The result is a single formula: **self = a Bayesian-mechanical attractor, individuated by a Markov blanket, whose "existence" just is the ongoing act of minimizing surprise about its own states.** This is what lets him give one mechanism for radically different phenomena — psychedelics, meditation, dissociation, flow, psychiatric symptom formation, even the absence of selfhood in LLMs — as different parameter settings of the same underlying process, rather than needing a separate theory for each. That unifying power is also exactly what his critics (Stegemann, Thompson) attack: the same three-model stack that produces the elegance also produces the unfalsifiability, the category-blur between physical and semantic description, and the a priori commitment to an organism-bound boundary that forecloses enactivist alternatives.

## EVOLUTION OVER THE WINDOW (Aug 2025 – Aug 2026)

The dossier's own synthesis names the key shift, and it's a **register change, not a framework change**. Two parallel threads run through the year:

1. **Solo/near-solo physics-register output** (Physics of Sentience talk, Physics of "As If" essay, IAI solo pieces) — stays in Bayesian-mechanics/renormalization vocabulary, increasingly defensive after the Stegemann exchanges (Nov–Dec 2025), using first-principles argumentation for *justification* as much as for *discovery*.
2. **Consciousness/meditation-facing collaborative work** (Beautiful Loop with Laukkonen & Chandaria; the May 2026 Precision Psychiatry talk naming meditation, dissociation, and flow explicitly) — borrows Buddhist and phenomenological vocabulary (śūnyatā, "epistemic depth," "toroidal epistemicity") to redescribe the *same* three primary models in first-person-friendly terms.

The underlying reasoning architecture (Bayesian + first-principles + systems) does not change across these threads — what changes is audience and vocabulary, and, under the Stegemann critique specifically, a visible shift from *generative* first-principles use (deriving new claims) toward *defensive* first-principles use (protecting existing claims via renormalization-group rigor-signaling).

A genuinely new application, rather than a new model, appears in the AI-agency thread (IWAI, Eye on AI, VERSES/Axiom, LeCun/Marcus exchanges): the same apparatus gets pointed at LLMs, with the Inversion/define-by-negation secondary model becoming newly prominent as the tool for that specific fight ("just a mapping between content and content").
